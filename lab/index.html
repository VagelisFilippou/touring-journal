Results — performance, cornering, ride

Steady state, driver on board, no aero. Many inputs are still estimates from books, drawings and forums (the car is in Greece and hasn't been measured yet), so compare setups with each other rather than trusting the absolute numbers. Road — time and speed on a real road

Pick the Voula hillclimb, or draw a route: click the start, any via points and the finish on the map.

Theoretical limit for the selected setup: full throttle, ideal gear changes, the cornering limit from the model in every bend and the braking limit before it; dry road, no driver margin. Roads come from OpenStreetMap (routing by OSRM), heights from Open-Meteo (Copernicus DEM, 90 m); the elevation is smoothed.
